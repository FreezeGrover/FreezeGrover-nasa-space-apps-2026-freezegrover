# Research sources

Consulted 14 November 2026. These are source references, not a claim that raw datasets were downloaded.

- Official challenge: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/2026/challenges/flame-in-freefall/ — title and objective found through indexed official content; direct fetch returned 502.
- NASA PSI research inventory: https://www.nasa.gov/physical-sciences-informatics-psi/psi-investigations-by-research-area/ — combustion investigation names.
- FLEX: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-69 — DOI 10.60555/mbq8-0451. Indexed NASA record describes liquid droplets, suppressants, and experimental approach. Client-rendered record was not readable through direct extraction.
- NASA overview: https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/studying-combustion-and-fire-safety/ — curated FLEX, FLEX-2, BASS-II summaries; source of cool-flame context.
- SAME-R: https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/data/investigations/PSI-101 — DOI 10.60555/2np7-jv14. Indexed NASA record describes smoke characterization and diagnostic changes.

No guessed PSI identifiers are assigned to FLEX-2 or BASS-II. Their links lead to the NASA overview until exact original records are checked. Numerical conditions, dimensions, and sensors not imported from original test files remain explicitly unavailable. Hypotheses are not treated as measured findings.

Rankings currently use curated topic tags. They are neither a NASA ranking nor a validated scientific scoring method. Lunar and Martian relevance requires gravity, atmosphere, geometry, and ventilation transfer analysis.
